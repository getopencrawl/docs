---
title: Import past sessions
description: Make earlier conversations on this machine searchable.
---

During setup, **Import past sessions** lists agents with history available to import. Select the agents whose past chats you want to share and choose **Import history**. You can skip this step and still record new chats.

![A stack of past chat transcripts moves into a tabbed index.](/images/docs/pixel/import-history.png)

Once a past chat appears in **Sessions**, ask your agent to find a phrase you remember from it.
