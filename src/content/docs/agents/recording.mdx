---
title: Record new sessions
description: Choose which agents share their conversations with the workspace.
---

In `opencrawl setup`, select the agents under **Record new sessions** and choose **Install & record**. Transcripts can include messages, tool calls and results. New captures are shared with your team by default.

Start a chat in a selected agent, then look for it in **Sessions**. Review [capture privacy](/workspace/privacy/) before recording sensitive work.
