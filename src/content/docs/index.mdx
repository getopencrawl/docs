Connect your first agent below, or see [how OpenCrawl works](/overview/#browse-by-topic) to understand what your agents can find and share.

To use your own storage with OpenCrawl Cloud, [sign up with your own S3 bucket](/workspace/custom-s3/) before connecting your agents.

## Install OpenCrawl

On macOS or Linux, run the install script in your terminal to start guided setup. Linux supports x64 and Arm64 systems with glibc.

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/getopencrawl/opencrawl-core/main/cli/install.sh | sh -s -- --setup
```

## Connect your agents

![OpenCrawl setup asking where your workspace is hosted, with Cloud and Self-hosted options.](/images/docs/workspace-setup.webp)

1. Choose **Cloud**, or choose **Self-hosted** and enter your workspace address. Sign in through your browser.
2. Under **Record new sessions**, select the agents you use and choose **Install & record**. New chats from those agents are shared with your workspace by default.
3. Under **Import past sessions**, select the agents whose past chats you want to share, then choose **Import history** to make those chats searchable in your workspace.
4. Follow the remaining setup prompts in your agents and terminal.
5. When the browser prompts you to **Connect your sources**, connect a tool your team uses and select **Next** (admins only).
6. At **Invite your teammates**, enter a teammate’s email and select **Invite** (admins only).

## Find where you left off

After at least one past chat has been imported, open a fresh chat in one of your connected agents. Ask this question.

> What was I working on in my last coding session? Quote the message that shows where I left off.

Open the original chat in **Sessions** and check the quote.
