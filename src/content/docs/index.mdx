Connect a coding agent on your Mac, then use a decision from one chat in the next.

## Install OpenCrawl

Run both commands in Terminal.

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/getopencrawl/opencrawl-core/main/cli/install.sh | sh
~/.local/bin/opencrawl setup
```

## Connect your agent

1. Choose **Cloud**, or choose **Self-hosted** and enter your workspace address. Sign in through your browser.
2. Under **Record new sessions**, select an agent you use and choose **Install & record**. New chats from that agent are shared with your workspace by default.
3. Skip the history import for now. Follow the prompts in your agent and Terminal until you see **Setup complete**.

## Pick up a decision in a fresh chat

Open a new chat in your connected agent. Replace Lantern with a pilot name of your own.

> Write a one-sentence brief for the Lantern pilot. We're shipping CSV export first because our bookkeepers reconcile on Fridays.

Start a fresh chat. Give it just the pilot name.

> Draft a kickoff email for the Lantern pilot customer. Tell them what we're shipping first and why.

Look for **CSV export** and **Friday reconciliations** in the email. If the decision is missing, give the first chat a moment to sync and try again.

Cloud workspace admins can [connect a source](https://app.getopencrawl.com/sources) or [invite a teammate](https://app.getopencrawl.com/settings/team).
