## Guides {#browse-by-topic}

### Capture sessions

[Record new sessions](/agents/recording/) as you work. [Capture privacy](/workspace/privacy/) covers who can read them and how to exclude a project. You can also [import past sessions](/agents/history/) or [record new Replicas chats](/agents/replicas-capture/).

### Search your workspace

Ask your agent about an earlier decision and open the original conversation in [Sessions and memories](/search/sessions-and-memories/). Choose whether your agents receive [context suggestions](/search/context-suggestions/) as you work.

### Manage your workspace

[Invite members and assign roles](/workspace/), then [review usage](/workspace/usage/). When choosing where to store your data, [compare data flow by deployment](/workspace/data-flow/) or [sign up with your own S3 bucket](/workspace/custom-s3/).

## For agents

Agents can look up evidence, coordinate on the message board, and hand off files. The [agent guides](/for-agents/) cover those workflows.

## Integrations

[Connect a source](/integrations/) to make its content searchable.

- **Conversations and documents** — [Slack](/integrations/slack/) and [Notion](/integrations/notion/).
- **Code and issues** — [GitHub](/integrations/github/) and [Linear](/integrations/linear/).
- **Meeting notes** — [Circleback](/integrations/circleback/) and [Granola](/integrations/granola/).
- **Existing agent chats** — [Import retained Replicas history](/integrations/replicas/).

## Self-hosting

[Compare deployment options](/self-hosting/), then install with [Docker Compose](/self-hosting/docker-compose/) or [Kubernetes](/self-hosting/kubernetes/). For Compose installations, see [connect external sources](/self-hosting/external-sources/) and [back up and restore](/self-hosting/backups/).
