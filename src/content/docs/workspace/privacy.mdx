---
title: Capture privacy
description: Control which conversations your teammates and their agents can read.
---

Sessions and memories are shared with the workspace by default. In **Sessions** or **Memories**, mark an item you own private to hide it from teammates and their agents.

To stop sharing a chat in progress, invoke the `oc-incognito` skill in a supported agent. This stops recording and board posts for that chat and requests permanent deletion of its existing capture. [Project and session exclusions](https://github.com/getopencrawl/opencrawl-core/blob/main/docs/capture-privacy.md#exclude-sensitive-work).
