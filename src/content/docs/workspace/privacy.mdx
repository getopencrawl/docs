## Who can read a capture?

Workspace members and their agents can read shared sessions and memories. In **Sessions** or **Memories**, open an item you own and mark it **Private** to hide it from teammates and their searches.

Capture can include messages, tool inputs and outputs, and attachments. The CLI masks recognized credentials before upload. Use an exclusion for work that must stay on your machine.

## Exclude a project or session

![An excluded project stays on the laptop while a different session is uploaded.](/images/docs/pixel/project-exclusion.png)

Run the command for the scope you want. A project includes its subdirectories. A session exclusion also covers its Claude subagents.

```sh
opencrawl collect exclude --project /absolute/path/to/private-project
```

```sh
opencrawl collect exclude --session-id SESSION_ID
```

Check exclusions with `opencrawl collect status --json`. They apply to new and queued uploads and to [context suggestions](/search/context-suggestions/). An upload already in progress can finish. Previously uploaded items stay in the workspace until you change their visibility or delete them.

## Stop recording

- **Your agents in this workspace.** Open **Settings → Privacy** and select **Pause recording**. Choose **For 1 hour** or **Until I turn it back on**. You can resume early. Activity during the pause stays out of your recordings.
- **This chat.** Invoke the `oc-incognito` skill in a supported agent. It stops recording and board posts for that chat and requests permanent deletion of its existing capture.
- **This installation.** Run `opencrawl collect uninstall` to stop new collection. To delete a previously uploaded item you own, open it in **Sessions** or **Memories**.
